## Description

ReadRabbit is a quiet reader for curious people.

Choose technology, economics, mathematics, or any combination of the three. ReadRabbit finds thoughtful writing from independent blogs and presents it in a focused, swipeable reader.

READ WITH LESS NOISE

Move through worthwhile articles without a crowded feed, trending counters, ads, or engagement traps.

FOLLOW YOUR CURIOSITY

Like a paragraph, bookmark an article, or move on. ReadRabbit learns from the choices you make while keeping you in control of your topics.

HEAR DIFFERENT VOICES

Recommendations are designed to avoid repetitive authors and publications, so one prolific source does not take over your reading list.

MAKE IT YOURS

Choose your topics, mute authors you do not want to see, keep bookmarks, and return to saved reading whenever you like.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

No account is required. Your article library, bookmarks, reading history, likes, muted authors, and preferences stay on your device. ReadRabbit uses limited anonymous product analytics to improve the app.

ReadRabbit is free and open source.

## Review notes

ReadRabbit is a local-first RSS reader that fetches articles from publicly available blogs and feeds. It does not require an account or login. Its article library, bookmarks, reading history, and preferences remain on the device.

BACKGROUND FETCH: ReadRabbit uses iOS Background App Refresh to periodically check feeds for new articles. This happens on the device. No personal data is sent during background refresh. iOS controls when the task runs.

THIRD-PARTY CONTENT: Articles displayed in the app are written by independent authors and fetched directly from their public feeds. ReadRabbit does not host or modify this content. It functions like an RSS reader or reading list.

ANALYTICS: ReadRabbit uses PostHog for anonymous product interaction events. It does not create person profiles, perform location enrichment, collect touch recordings, or use IDFA. Its random analytics identifier is session-scoped and discarded when the app restarts.

SCREENSHOT CONTENT: The article titled "The Shape of a Useful Question," credited to Mara Vale and The Quiet Index, is entirely fictional. It was written by the ReadRabbit development team solely to demonstrate the reader interface in App Store screenshots. It is not copied from or attributed to a real author, publication, or website.

NO ACCOUNT REQUIRED: ReadRabbit has no login, registration, or account system. All reading data is stored on the device.

TO TEST: Open the app, select one or more topics, and tap "Start reading." ReadRabbit will begin fetching articles over the network. Articles will appear in the swipe reader after the initial refresh completes.
